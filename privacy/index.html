Trove Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Trove is a personal memory app operated by Zyphr (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy explains what information Trove handles, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information Trove handles

  • Account information: your email address and account identifier. Sign-in credentials are checked by Supabase Auth; Trove does not store your raw password in its application database.
  • Memories and files: text, links, notes, titles, capture dates, Spaces, and metadata you submit, along with attachments such as images, audio, video, and other files.
  • App and device data: session information stored securely on your device, local captures waiting to sync, and preferences needed to operate the app.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate you, save and sync your memories, organize and search your library, provide exports, prevent unauthorized access, and respond to support requests. Trove does not display third-party ads or sell personal information.

When embeddings are enabled for an account, Trove sends relevant memory text or images, and semantic-search query text, to Google Cloud Vertex AI to create embeddings. Trove uses those embeddings for semantic search and related-memory features. Trove does not use this service to generate written answers or summaries.

When you save a link, Trove may fetch that URL to extract page information for the saved memory. The website may receive a standard network request from Trove’s servers.

Service providers

Trove uses Supabase for authentication, database, and private file storage; Heroku to host the Trove API; and Google Cloud Vertex AI for embeddings when that feature is enabled. These providers process information as needed to deliver their services to Trove. Trove does not provide provider storage URLs or credentials to the mobile app.

Storage, security, and retention

Memory files are stored in private storage and delivered through authenticated Trove API requests. Network connections to Trove and its providers use HTTPS/TLS. Access controls help restrict account data to its owner. Trove is not end-to-end encrypted: authorized Trove services and the providers needed to operate the app process data to provide its features.

Your memories are retained while your account is active. You can export your data from Profile or request account deletion in the app. Deletion is scheduled seven days after the request; you can cancel it during that period. Afterward, Trove removes the account’s database records and stored files and requests deletion of its Supabase Auth account. Trove retains a minimal account-subject record to reject still-valid tokens and make deletion retries safe; it does not contain your email or memory content. Hosting providers may retain limited operational records under their own retention practices.

Your choices

You can sign out, export your data, and request deletion from the app. Local captures that have not yet synced remain on your device until they can be delivered or you remove them locally. For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@zyphrlabs.com.

Children

Trove is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to Trove, contact us at hello@zyphrlabs.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Trove’s features or data practices change. The effective date above shows when it was last revised.
